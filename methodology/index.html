Methodology

Corrected points are calculated as a team's total points divided by their games played. This is then multiplied by 22 (the number of games in a 1st XI season. This makes all team's have a points total that can be compared across standards and divisions.

The points are then corrected for the division they were gained in. This means that a 1st division point is worth about half a premier division point. A 2nd division point is worth approximately half a 1st division point and so on.

A corrected point is worth the same in a 1st team, 2nd team or 3rd team.

A club's best performing 3rd XI is chosen to contribute to the club score. It was found that an average of all a club's 3rd XIs often penalised clubs who were able to put out multiple 3rd XIs which seemed unfair. Clicking the details button on a clubs points in the table will display which teams contributed to the total club score.
